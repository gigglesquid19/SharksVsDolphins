Sharks Vs Dolphins

Swim, dodge, and recruit your pod to survive the open ocean.

Far below the waves lies the Endless Blue, a vast ocean where dolphin pods once travelled freely between feeding grounds, coral cities, and deep-water sanctuaries.

Then one day everything changed...

The sharks began hunting in organized packs, driving dolphins from their territories and scattering pods across the ocean. Small groups of dolphins became isolated, vulnerable, and easy prey. Fear spread through the sea.

You are Echo, a young dolphin separated from your pod during a shark attack.

SharksVsDolphins

Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen D-pad to swim. On mobile you can also touch and hold anywhere on the water; the dolphin swims in that direction relative to the centre of the screen. Swim off the left or right edge to wrap around, Pac-Man style. Find lost dolphins to recruit them to your pod. Each dolphin is a life; if a shark catches you, one dolphin is lost. Once your pod reaches 5 dolphins, you enter HUNTING MODE and can destroy sharks by ramming them.
