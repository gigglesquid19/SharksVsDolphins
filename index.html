About Me

I’m a Marine Biologist turned Software Developer from the UK, with a lifelong love for the ocean and a passion for marine conservation.

I created Sharks vs Dolphins as an indie project, combining my love of the sea with my interest in game development. The ocean faces many growing threats, and with limited funding available for meaningful conservation work, every little bit can help. If you enjoy the game, I’d be incredibly grateful if you considered making a small donation to a marine conservation organisation.

As an indie developer, we pour a huge amount of passion, time and effort into our projects while competing with much larger studios. I also try to keep the game experience enjoyable without relying heavily on intrusive ads.

If you’ve enjoyed the game and would like to buy me a coffee, it genuinely helps support future updates and new projects. ☕🌊

I always welcome feedback! If you have ideas for improvements, something you didn’t like, suggestions for future features, or if you find a bug, please get in touch:

Far below the waves lies the Endless Blue, an ocean where dolphin pods once ran freely between the feeding grounds, the coral cities and the deep-water sanctuaries.

Then the sharks learned to hunt together.

They came in packs, driving the pods off the reefs and scattering them into open water. Forty became four. Songs went out across the dark and came back unanswered.

You are Echo, separated from your pod on a night like that one. Keep out of sight, find the ones still out there, and take the Shallows back one depth at a time.

SharksVsDolphins

Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen D-pad to swim. On mobile you can also touch and hold anywhere on the water; the dolphin swims in that direction relative to the centre of the screen. Swim off the left or right edge to wrap around, Pac-Man style. Find lost dolphins to recruit them to your pod. Each dolphin is a life; if a shark catches you, one dolphin is lost. Once your pod reaches 4 dolphins, you enter HUNTING MODE and can destroy sharks by ramming them. Tap the middle of the water to Boost, and tap it twice for Echolocation - the rings around your dolphin show when each is charged.
