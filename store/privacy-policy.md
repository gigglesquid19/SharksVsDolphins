Privacy Policy — Sharks vs Dolphins
==================================

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This privacy policy applies to the **Sharks vs Dolphins** mobile game ("the
app", package `com.sharksvsdolphins.app`) published on Google Play by
**[YOUR NAME / STUDIO NAME]** ("we", "us").

Short version
-------------
The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account system, ads, or
analytics. Everything it saves stays on your device.

Information we collect
----------------------
**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or
sensitive information. It has no login, no user accounts, and makes no network
requests to us or to any third party while you play.

Information stored on your device
---------------------------------
The app saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device using
standard browser local storage, so your progress and settings persist between
sessions:

- high-score leaderboard entries and the 3-letter initials you enter for them;
- which one-time achievements and tutorial tips you have unlocked or seen;
- audio volume / mute and control-scheme (D-pad or joystick) preferences;
- a single in-progress Campaign checkpoint so a run can be resumed.

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to us, and we cannot
see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third-party services
--------------------
The app contains **no** third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics, or
crash-reporting services. Bundled assets (music, artwork) are packaged inside
the app and are not loaded from any external server at runtime.

Permissions
-----------
The app declares the `INTERNET` permission. It is required by the app framework
to load the game's own bundled files into an in-app web view; the app does not
use it to contact any external server. No other permissions are requested — no
location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or device identifiers.

Children's privacy
------------------
The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

"Last updated" date above will change.
