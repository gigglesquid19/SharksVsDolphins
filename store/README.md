store/
======

Google Play submission material. Not shipped in the app.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `listing.md` | App name, short + full description, category, Data Safety answers, content-rating answers, App-content declarations, and a step-by-step release checklist. Fill the `[BRACKETS]`. |
| `privacy-policy.md` | Ready-to-host privacy policy. Fill name/email/date, publish it somewhere public, put the URL in the listing. |
| `icon-512.png` | 512×512 Play Store listing icon. |
| `feature-graphic.png` | 1024×500 feature graphic. |
| `screenshots/HOW-TO-CAPTURE.md` | Which screens to capture on the Pixel and the Play spec. |

Everything here is generated from `Images/GameLogo.jpg` and the app itself;
regenerate the graphics with `tools/generate_store_assets.py`.
