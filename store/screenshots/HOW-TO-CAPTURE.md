Phone screenshots
=================

Play needs **2–8 phone screenshots**. Spec: PNG or JPG, 16:9 or 9:16, each side
320–3840 px. A Pixel screenshot (e.g. 1080×2400) is already perfect — just take
them on the device.

Capture these on the Pixel (portrait), from a fresh `npm run build:android`
sideload so they show the current build:

| # | Screen | How to get there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title screen** | Launch the app. Clean shot of the logo + Campaign / Endless buttons. |
| 2 | **Early gameplay** | Campaign, level 1. A moment with the dolphin, a couple of sharks, and one or two pod dolphins visible. |
| 3 | **Hunting Mode** | Play until the pod hits 5 and Hunting Mode triggers (the banner shows). Shoot during the ram. |
| 4 | **A storm** | Wait for a procedural storm (screen dims, vision shrinks) and capture mid-storm. |
| 5 | **Matriarch / Mega Pod** | Reach level 10 and capture the Matriarch fight, ideally with the Mega Pod called in. |
| 6 (optional) | **Leaderboard** | Finish a run, enter initials, capture the Campaign/Endless leaderboard tabs. |

Tips:
- Turn on a full pod and dramatic action — screenshots 3 and 5 are the ones
  that sell the game.
- Take a few extra of each and pick the best 4–6.
- Drop the final files in this folder (`store/screenshots/`) named `01-...`,
  `02-...` etc. so the order is obvious when uploading.
- If you want matching device-frame mockups later, Play's listing accepts raw
  screenshots fine — framing is optional polish.
